# HALO:AUnifiedVision-Language-Action Model for Embodied Multimodal Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
# HALO：面向具身多模态思维链推理的统一视觉 - 语言 - 动作模型
## Abstract
Vision–Language–Action (VLA) models have shown strong performance in robotic manipulation, but often
struggle in **long-horizon** or **out-of-distribution scenarios** due to the lack of explicit mechanisms for multimodal
reasoning and anticipating how the world will evolve under action. Recent works introduce textual chain-of
thought or visual subgoal prediction within VLA models to reason, but still fail to offer a unified human-like
reasoning framework（类人推理框架） for joint **textual reasoning, visual foresight（前瞻）, and action prediction**. To this end, we propose
HALO, a unified VLA model that enables **embodied multimodal chain-of-thought** (EM-CoT) reasoning through a
sequential process（串行（顺序）流程） of textual task reasoning, visual subgoal（子目标） prediction for fine-grained（细粒度） guidance, and EM-CoT
augmented（增强版） action prediction. We instantiate（实例化） HALO with a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) architecture（架构） that
decouples（解耦） semantic reasoning（语义推理）, visual foresight, and action prediction into specialized experts（专用专家） while allowing
seamless cross-expert collaboration. To enable HALO learning at scale, we introduce an **automated pipeline**（自动化流水线） to
synthesize EM-CoT training data along with a carefully crafted training recipe（范式）. Extensive experiments demonstrate
that: (1) HALO achieves superior performance in both simulated and real-world environments, surpassing baseline
policy π0 by 34.1% on **RoboTwin** benchmark; (2) all proposed components of the training recipe and EM-CoT
design help improve task success rate; and (3) HALO exhibits strong generalization capabilities under aggressive
unseen environmental randomization with our proposed EM-CoT reasoning

视觉 - 语言 - 动作（VLA）模型已在机器人操作任务中展现出优异性能，但由于缺乏显式的多模态推理机制，且无法预判动作执行下的环境演化规律，这类模型在长时序任务或**分布外场景**中往往表现不佳。现有研究通过在 VLA 模型中引入文本思维链或视觉子目标预测实现推理能力，但仍未能构建一套可联合实现**文本推理、视觉前瞻与动作预测**的统一类人推理框架。针对这一问题，本文提出 HALO—— 一种统一的 VLA 模型，该模型通过 “文本任务推理→提供细粒度引导的视觉子目标预测→具身多模态思维链增强的动作预测” 的串行流程，实现了  **具身多模态思维链（Embodied Multimodal Chain-of-Thought, EM-CoT）推理。本文采用混合 Transformer（Mixture-of-Transformers, MoT）** 架构实现 HALO，该架构将语义推理、视觉前瞻与动作预测解耦为多个专用专家模块，同时实现了跨专家模块的无缝协同。为支撑 HALO 的规模化学习，本文设计了一套自动化 EM-CoT 训练数据合成管线，以及配套的精细化训练范式。大量实验结果表明：(1) HALO 在仿真与真实世界环境中均取得了领先性能，在 RoboTwin 基准测试集上相较基线策略 π₀提升了 34.1%；(2) 本文提出的训练范式各组件与 EM-CoT 核心设计，均能有效提升任务成功率；(3) 借助本文提出的 EM-CoT 推理机制，HALO 在严苛的未知环境随机化条件下仍具备极强的泛化能力。

## 1. Introduction
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models (Black et al., 2024; NVIDIA et al., 2025) have shown a compelling path toward
general-purpose（通用用途） robotic manipulation（操作）. However, most existing VLAs map perceptual inputs directly to motor commands,
lacking explicit mechanisms（显示机制） for reasoning about task structure or anticipating（预测） how the environment will evolve（变化） under motion
and contact（接触）. This limitation becomes particularly pronounced in long-horizon or out-of-distribution scenarios—such as
novel layouts, unfamiliar objects, or contact-rich interactions（相互作用）—where successful execution（判决方式） depends more on deliberation（审议）
and foresight than on reactive pattern matching（模式匹配）.

视觉 - 语言 - 动作（VLA）模型（Black 等人，2024；NVIDIA 团队，2025）已为通用机器人操作指明了极具潜力的发展方向。但现有绝大多数 VLA 模型仅将感知输入直接映射为电机指令，缺乏用于任务结构推理、以及预判运动与接触交互下环境演化规律的**显式机制**。这一局限在长时序任务或分布外场景中尤为突出 —— 例如全新的场景布局、陌生物体、或是高接触交互任务；在这类场景中，任务能否成功执行，更多取决于审慎推理与前瞻预判能力，而非反应式的模式匹配。

Recent work has sought to address this limitation by introducing **intermediate reasoning processes like human**（类人的中间推理方式）. Inspired（受...的启发）
by the success of Chain-of-Thought reasoning (Wei et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025a;b) in large language models, Zawalski
et al. (2025) train VLAs to perform multi-step reasoning（多步推理） prior（在..前） to action prediction, **enabling task decomposition（任务分解）** **but** without
grounding such reasoning in **explicit visual state evolution（显示视觉状态演化）**. In contrast, Zhao et al. (2025) **autoregressively（自回归）** generate **subgoal
images（子目标图像）** as visual intermediate steps, but **lack semantic reasoning capabilities（缺乏语义推理能力）**. Achieving human-like textual reasoning and
visual imagination capability within a unified framework remains challenging, as it demands strong **multimodal generation（多模态生成）**
while preserving reasoning capacity（同时保留推理能力）. Existing approaches (Zhao et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2025) often tightly couple（结合/耦合） visual
generation with VLMs via **discrete visual token prediction（离散视觉令牌预测）**, which may hinder（阻碍） rich understanding capabilities of VLMs.
Consequently, a unified architecture（架构） that jointly supports multimodal reasoning, visual generation, and action prediction
remains（仍是） an open problem.

近期研究通过引入类人的中间推理过程，试图解决这一局限。受思维链（Chain-of-Thought）推理在大语言模型中取得成功的启发（Wei 等人，2023；Chen 等人，2025a;b），Zawalski 等人（2025）对 VLA 模型进行训练，使其在动作预测前完成多步推理，实现了任务分解，但并未将该推理过程锚定到显式的视觉状态演化中。与之相反，Zhao 等人（2025）通过自回归方式生成子目标图像作为视觉中间步骤，却缺乏语义推理能力。如何在统一框架内同时实现类人的文本推理与视觉想象能力，目前仍是一项挑战 —— 这要求模型在保留推理能力的同时，具备强大的多模态生成能力。现有方法（Zhao 等人，2025；Gu 等人，2025）通常通过**离散视觉令牌预测**，将视觉生成模块与视觉语言模型（VLM）紧耦合，这一设计可能会损害 VLM 原本强大的深层理解能力。因此，能够同时支持多模态推理、视觉生成与动作预测的统一架构，仍是该领域尚未解决的开放问题。

> #### 细粒度引导
> 不是只给 “大概方向”，而是给非常精确、细节拉满、一步到位的指引，让机器人知道具体要做到什么样子、看到什么画面才算完成。
>
> 在机器人、计算机视觉、具身智能里：
> - 粗粒度（coarse-grained）：只给目标，比如 “把杯子放到桌上”，没有细节。
> - 细粒度（fine-grained）：给出位置、姿态、外观、空间关系、时序步骤等精确信息。
> 细粒度引导 = 用高度具体、可直接执行的细节来指导动作。

To address this, we propose HALO, a unified VLA model that enables embodied multimodal chain-of-thought (EM-CoT)
reasoning. As illustrated in Figure 1, HALO first performs textual reasoning and task planning, then predicts subgoal
images to provide fine-grained visual guidance, and finally uses the resulting EM-CoT as context for action prediction.
This design is specifically engineered（设计） to emulate（模拟） the human cognitive process of deliberate（深思熟虑） reasoning, mental imagination,
and subsequent execution（执行）, rendering HALO particularly well-suited for tackling complex, long-horizon tasks that require
intricate（复杂） sequential（序列） reasoning and sustained（维持） contextual understanding over extended periods（长时间的）.

为了解决这一问题，我们提出了HALO，一个统一的视觉-语言-动作模型，能够进行具身多模态思维链推理。如图1所示，HALO首先进行文本推理和任务规划，然后预测子目标图像以提供细粒度的视觉指引，最后将生成的EM-CoT作为上下文用于动作预测。这一设计专门用于模仿人类先深思熟虑、再心理想象、最后执行操作的认知过程，使HALO特别适合应对那些需要复杂序列推理和长时间持续语境理解的长期程任务。

HALO incorporates（合并，融合） several key innovations to realize this vision. First, to natively support embodied multimodal reasoning,
HALO adopts a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) architecture (Liang et al., 2024) that decouples（解耦） textual reasoning, visual
foresight, and action prediction into three specialized experts. These experts collaborate（协作） seamlessly（无缝衔接地） through shared self
attention, while preserving（保存） each expert’s natural generative workflow—autoregressive text generation（工作流——自回归文本生成） for reasoning and
diffusion-based（基于扩散的） prediction for visual states and actions—thereby avoiding potential conflicts（潜在冲突） that arise when heterogeneous（异构性，异质性）
capabilities are forced into a single monolithic（单体式） model. Second, we introduce an automatic EM-CoT data synthesis（自动化EM-cot数据合成） pipeline
built upon action primitives（原语） and VLM annotator（注释器，标注器）. The pipeline performs task planning first and then decomposes（分解） long-horizon
robot trajectories（轨迹） into a sequence（序列） of sub-tasks, each paired（配对） with a corresponding（对应的） subgoal image and explicit reasoning
process. This enables automatic and scalable（可拓展性的） embodied multimodal reasoning supervision（监督） for our unified VLA model. Third,
we propose a carefully designed training recipe that combines broad generalization with embodied reasoning specialization.
(1) In a versatile（多功能） pre-training stage, HALO is trained on diverse carefully crafted（制作） data sources, including general VQA
datasets for robust（稳健的） multimodal understanding, egocentric（自我中心的） and robotic video datasets（数据集） for learning contact-rich（接触丰富的） physical
dynamics（物理动力学）, and heterogeneous（异构） robot trajectory（轨迹） datasets for acquiring（掌握） foundational（基础） manipulation skills. (2) In a subsequent
EM-CoT–augmented fine-tuning（微调） stage, the model is co-trained（联合训练） on EM-CoT and VQA data to inject（注入） EM-CoT reasoning
capability while preserving its general world knowledge.

HALO为实现这一愿景融合了几项关键创新。首先，为原生支持具身多模态推理，HALO采用了混合专家Transformer架构，将文本推理、视觉预见和动作预测解耦为三个专门的专家模块。这些专家通过共享自注意力机制无缝协作，同时各自**保留其自然的生成工作流**——推理采用**自回归文本生成**，视觉状态和动作则采用**基于扩散的预测**——从而避免了将**异构能力强行塞入**单一整体模型所带来的潜在冲突。

其次，我们引入了一条基于**动作基元**和VLM标注器的自动化EM-CoT数据合成管线。该管线首先进行任务规划，然后将长程机器人轨迹分解为一系列子任务，每个子任务均配有相应的子目标图像和明确的推理过程，从而为我们的统一VLA模型提供自动化且可扩展的具身多模态推理监督。

第三，我们设计了一套将广泛泛化能力与具身推理专长相结合的精心训练方案：（1）在通用预训练阶段，HALO在多种精心构建的数据源上进行训练，包括用于培养鲁棒多模态理解能力的通用视觉问答数据集、用于学习接触丰富物理动态的自我中心和机器人视频数据集，以及用于获取基础操作技能的异构机器人轨迹数据集；（2）在随后的EM-CoT增强微调阶段，模型在EM-CoT和VQA数据上进行联合训练，以注入EM-CoT推理能力，同时保持其通用的世界知识。

We conduct extensive（广泛的） experiments（实验） in both the RoboTwin 2.0 simulator (Chen et al., 2025c) and real-world environments to
validate （验证）the effectiveness（效能） of HALO. The results demonstrate that HALO achieves consistently（持续稳定的） stronger overall performance
across both simulated and real-world settings, establishing a new state-of-the-art with an average success rate of 80.5% on
RoboTwin, surpassing π0 by 34%. In addition, we perform comprehensive ablation studies to verify: (1) the effectiveness
of the proposed EM-CoT, showing that combining textual reasoning with visual foresight contribute significantly to
performance; and (2) the importance of each category of pre-training tasks, all of which provide measurable benefits to
downstream performance. We further demonstrate the strong generalization capability of HALO in both simulation and real
robots, and qualitatively show that it can generate reasonable EM-CoT to guide action prediction in unseen scenarios. Taken
together, these results highlight HALO as a scalable and generalizable paradigm for human-like VLA reasoning.

我们在**RoboTwin 2.0模拟器**和真实世界环境中进行了大量实验，以验证HALO的有效性。结果表明，HALO在模拟和真实场景中均始终展现出更强的整体性能，在RoboTwin上以80.5%的平均成功率建立了新的最优水平，超越π0达34个百分点。此外，我们进行了全面的消融研究，验证了：(1) 所提出的EM-CoT的有效性，表明文本推理与视觉预见的结合对性能贡献显著；(2) 每一类预训练任务的重要性，它们都为下游性能带来了可测量的提升。我们进一步展示了HALO在模拟和真实机器人上的强大泛化能力，并定性地表明该模型能够在未见场景中生成合理的EM-CoT来指导动作预测。综合来看，这些结果凸显了HALO作为一种可扩展且可泛化的类人VLA推理范式的重要价值。

## 2. Related Work
**Vision-Language-Action Models**. Predominant VLA models are built on top of Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
pre-trained on large-scale web data, inheriting rich multimodal knowledge about the world. Representative architectures
such as OpenVLA (O’Neill et al., 2024) and FAST (Pertsch et al., 2025) directly extend pre-trained VLMs by introducing
discrete action tokens and predicting actions autoregressively to solve embodied tasks. The π0 series (Black et al., 2024;
Intelligence et al., 2025) further introduce a separate action expert along with the pre-trained VLM for flow-based action
prediction, achieving better performance and efficiency. Beyond equipping VLM with only action prediction capability,
unified VLA architectures (Bi et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025; Cen et al., 2025; Lv et al., 2025) have been proposed to
enhance dynamic awareness by jointly modeling textual understanding, visual generation, and action prediction. These
methods incorporate image or video generation components (Zhu et al., 2025; 2026) to predict future environmental states,
using either unified (Cen et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025) or mixed (Bi et al., 2025; Lv et al., 2025) architectures that are
trained together with action prediction to capture underlying environment dynamics. However, these methods lack explicit
textual or visual reasoning capability, limiting their performance in complex and dynamic environments.

视觉-语言-动作模型。当前主流的VLA模型构建在大规模网络数据预训练的视觉-语言模型之上，继承了关于世界的丰富多模态知识。代表性架构如OpenVLA和FAST直接扩展了预训练的VLM，通过**引入离散动作词元**并以**自回归方式预测动作**来解决具身任务。π0系列则进一步引入了一个独立的动作专家，与预训练的VLM协同进行**基于流匹配**的动作预测，取得了更好的性能和效率。除了仅赋予VLM动作预测能力之外，统一VLA架构已被提出用于通过联合建模文本理解、视觉生成和动作预测来增强动态感知能力。这些方法融合了图像或视频生成组件来预测未来环境状态，采用与动作预测共同训练的统一架构或混合架构来捕捉底层环境动态。**然而，这些方法缺乏显式的文本或视觉推理能力，限制了它们在复杂和动态环境中的表现**。

**Textual and Visual Reasoning for Robotic Intelligence**. Reasoning is a crucial step towards robotic intelligence (LeCun,
2022). Motivated by the success of Chain-of-Thought reasoning in language models, recent work extends explicit reasoning
mechanisms to embodied decision making. MoTVLA (Bi et al., 2025) proposes a textual reasoning framework that performs
planning and reasoning prior to action execution. UP-VLA (Zhang et al., 2025), CoTVLA (Zhao et al., 2025) and InternVLA
A1 (Cai et al., 2026) further introduce visual reasoning by generating goal images and videos to guide downstream action
prediction. More recently, ManualVLA (Gu et al., 2025) tries to combine visual and textual reasoning through a shared
planning expert that produces both textual instructions and visual subgoals. However, this design couples image and text
generation within a single autoregressive model, which may degrade the reasoning capability of the underlying VLM. In
contrast, our proposed embodied multimodal chain-of-thought (EM-CoT) employs a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)
architecture for joint textual reasoning, visual generation, and action prediction. Our decoupled design enables more
effective reasoning across visual and textual modalities before executing actions.

#### 面向机器人智能的文本与视觉推理

推理是实现机器人智能的核心环节（LeCun, 2022）。受思维链推理在语言模型中取得成功的启发，现有研究已将显式推理机制拓展至具身决策领域。MoTVLA（Bi 等人，2025）提出了一种文本推理框架，可在动作执行前完成规划与推理流程。UP-VLA（Zhang 等人，2025）、CoTVLA（Zhao 等人，2025）与 InternVLA A1（Cai 等人，2026）进一步引入视觉推理机制，通过**生成目标图像与视频引导下游动作预测**。
近期，ManualVLA（Gu 等人，2025）尝试通过一个共享规划专家模块融合视觉与文本推理，该模块可同时生成文本指令与视觉子目标。然而，该设计将图像与文本生成耦合在单一自回归模型中，可能会削弱底层视觉语言模型（Vision Language Model, VLM）的推理能力。与之不同，本文提出的具身多模态思维链（embodied multimodal chain-of-thought, EM-CoT）采用混合 Transformer 架构（Mixture-of-Transformers, MoT），实现文本推理、视觉生成与动作预测的联合建模。这种解耦设计能够在动作执行前，实现跨视觉与文本模态的更高效推理。

## 3. Method

In this section, we delineate the core design principles of HALO, focusing on its unified architecture, the embodied
multimodal chain-of-thought (EM-CoT) data pipeline, and the associated training recipe. Collectively, these components
empower the model to engage in textual reasoning, visual foresight, and grounded action prediction.

在本节中，我们将阐述 HALO 的核心设计原则，重点介绍其统一架构、具身多模态思维链（EM-CoT）数据管线，以及配套的训练方案。上述组件协同作用，赋予模型文本推理、视觉前瞻与具身锚定动作预测的能力。

## 3.1. Problem Formulation

We formulate robotic manipulation as a sequential decision-making problem. Let $\tau = \{(o_t, l, a_t)\}_{t=1}^T$ denote a trajectory, comprising visual observations $o_t \in O$, language instructions $l \in L$, and continuous actions $a_t \in A$. Traditional VLA models typically learn a monolithic policy $\pi_\theta(a_{t:t+m} \mid l, o_{t-k:t})$ that directly maps history observations and instructions to action chunks. Such purely reactive policies often suffer from performance degradation when facing long-horizon or complex manipulation tasks due to a lack of intermediate reasoning.

In this case, we aim to integrate explicit EM-CoT as a robust conditioning mechanism in HALO and make its generation process align with typical human cognitive pathway of “thinking-imagination-execution”. To achieve our goal, we decompose the generation process of HALO into three distinct phases: textual reasoning, visual foresight, and action prediction. Then the problem is transformed into learning a unified policy $\pi_\theta$ that jointly models the following mappings:

$$
r \sim P_\theta(\cdot \mid l, o_{t-k:t})
$$

$$
\hat{o}_{t+h} \sim P_\theta(\cdot \mid l, o_{t-k:t}, r)
$$

$$
a_{t:t+m} \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid l, o_{t-k:t}, r, \hat{o}_{t+h})
$$

where $r$ represents generated textual chain-of-thought and $\hat{o}_{t+h}$ denotes predicted visual subgoal. Under this formulation, the action chunk $a_{t:t+m}$ is not a simple reaction, but the result of a deliberate reasoning trace and physical world modeling, ensuring that every control command is strategically planned and visually grounded.

## 3.2. Unified Architecture

We now present the unified architecture of HALO, as illustrated in Figure 1. Drawing inspiration from the capability of
BAGEL(Deng et al., 2025) to harmonize multimodal tasks, we adopt a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) (Liang et al., 2024)
architecture comprising three specialized experts: Multimodal Understanding, Visual Generation, and Action Prediction.
These three experts specialize in distinct modalities and are responsible for textual reasoning, visual generation, and
action prediction, respectively. While maintaining independent parameter sets, they interact through a shared self-attention
mechanism to enable rich cross-modal interactions. With such an architecture, HALO operates as a unified VLA model that
simultaneously possesses the capability to generate detailed textual reasoning and to produce explanatory subgoal images.

本节我们将详细介绍 HALO 的统一架构，整体结构如图 1 所示。受 BAGEL 模型（Deng 等人，2025）多模态任务协同处理能力的启发，我们采用了混合 Transformer 架构（Mixture-of-Transformers, MoT）（Liang 等人，2024），该架构包含三个专用专家模块：多模态理解模块、视觉生成模块与动作预测模块。
这三个专家模块针对不同模态做专项优化，分别对应承担文本推理、视觉生成与动作预测三大核心任务。各模块在保有独立参数集的同时，通过共享自注意力机制完成信息交互，从而实现丰富的跨模态信息联动。基于这一架构，HALO 可作为一个统一的视觉语言动作（VLA）模型，同时具备生成精细化文本推理结果与可解释性子目标图像的能力。


The switching mechanism between modalities is explicitly controlled via special tokens. By default, the model operates
as an auto-regressive planner; however, the generation of specific tokens (e.g., ⟨visual start⟩ or ⟨action start⟩) triggers the
routing of hidden states to the visual generation expert or the action prediction expert, respectively. In order to manage the
information flow among experts, we implement a carefully structured masking strategy within the shared self-attention. As
illustrated in Figure 3, a causal mask is applied to textual tokens to enforce autoregressive generation. Conversely, visual
tokens employ bidirectional attention within each frame to capture global spatial dependencies, while maintaining causal
masking for interactions across frames or modalities. Crucially, to prevent information leakage, noise tokens are restricted
from attending to their corresponding ground-truth targets. Furthermore, all other tokens are masked from attending to noise
tokens, ensuring they only aggregate information from valid non-noise contexts.

不同模态间的切换机制通过**特殊 token** 进行显式控制。默认情况下，模型以**自回归规划器的模式运行**；而当生成特定 token（如⟨visual start⟩或⟨action start⟩）时，会分别触发隐藏状态向视觉生成专家模块、动作预测专家模块的路由。

为管控各专家模块间的信息流，我们在共享自注意力机制中设计并实现了一套结构严谨的**掩码策略**。如图 3 所示，我们对文本 token 施加因果掩码，以严格约束自回归生成过程。与之相反，视觉 token 在单帧内部采用**双向注意力**，以捕捉全局空间依赖关系，同时在跨帧、跨模态的交互中仍保持因果掩码约束。

关键的是，为避免信息泄露，我们限制噪声 token 无法关注其对应的真值目标。此外，我们对所有其他 token 施加掩码，使其无法关注噪声 token，确保这些 token 仅从有效的非噪声上下文中聚合信息。


To facilitate training stability, we initialize each expert using the Qwen2.5-1.5B LLM (Yang et al., 2025), yielding a
cumulative parameter count of approximately 4.5B. To project heterogeneous data into a unified representation space,
we employ modality-specific encoders. For text, we adopt the standard Qwen2.5 tokenizer (Bai et al., 2023), expanding
the vocabulary with special control tokens—specifically ⟨think start⟩, ⟨vision start⟩, and ⟨action start⟩, among others—to
strictly demarcate the textual reasoning, visual generation, and acting phases, thereby enforcing a structured and interpretable
workflow. In the vision domain, we decouple the encoding mechanisms for semantic understanding and visual generation to
address their distinct functional requirements. For the understanding task, we employ a ViT (Dosovitskiy, 2020) initialized
with SigLIP2 (Tschannen et al., 2025) to leverage robust semantic features. To accommodate diverse real-world inputs
without geometric distortion, this encoder is further enhanced with NaViT (Dehghani et al., 2023), enabling the processing
of images in their native aspect ratios. For the visual generation task, we utilize a pre-trained VAE (Kingma & Welling,
2013; Labs et al., 2025) optimized for high-fidelity image compression and reconstruction. Finally, for the action modality,
given its low-dimensional and physically structured nature, a linear projection suffices to map actions into the model’s
hidden dimension while preserving intrinsic structural information. See Appendix A for further architectural details.

为提升训练稳定性，我们采用 Qwen2.5-1.5B 大语言模型（LLM）（Yang 等人，2025）对每个专家模块进行**权重初始化**，模型累计参数量约为 45 亿。为将**异构数据映射至统一的表征空间**，我们采用了面向不同模态的专属编码器。

针对文本模态，我们采用标准的 **Qwen2.5 分词器**（Bai 等人，2023），并通过特殊控制 token（具体包括⟨think start⟩、⟨vision start⟩、⟨action start⟩等）拓展词表，以此严格划分文本推理、视觉生成与动作执行阶段，确保工作流的结构化与可解释性。

在视觉模态中，我们针对语义理解与视觉生成两类任务的差异化功能需求，对二者的编码机制进行了解耦设计。针对语义理解任务，我们采用以 SigLIP2（Tschannen 等人，2025）预训练权重初始化的视觉 Transformer（Vision Transformer, ViT）（Dosovitskiy, 2020），以提取鲁棒的语义特征。为适配多样化的真实世界输入同时避免**几何畸变**，我们进一步引入原生分辨率视觉 Transformer（NaViT）（Dehghani 等人，2023）对该编码器进行增强，使其能够处理保持原始宽高比的图像。针对视觉生成任务，我们采用经过**高保真图像压缩与重建优化**的预训练变分自编码器（Variational Auto-Encoder, VAE）（Kingma & Welling, 2013; Labs 等人，2025）。

最后，针对动作模态，考虑到其低维、具备物理结构化的特性，仅通过线性投影层即可将动作映射至模型的隐藏维度，同时完整保留其内在结构信息。更多架构细节详见附录 A。

> #### 异构数据
> 异构数据（Heterogeneous Data）是相对于同构数据而言的基础概念，核心指来源、数据类型、组织结构、特征维度、语义内涵、统计分布存在本质差异，无法直接用单一模型 / 框架原生统一处理的多源数据集合，是数据科学、人工智能、数据库领域的核心研究对象。
> 
> 我们日常接触的文本、图片、视频、数值表格、传感器信号等，都属于典型的异构数据；而同一格式、同一结构、同一来源的数据（比如同一张表里的用户信息、同一文件夹里的同分辨率图片），则属于同构数据。
>
> #### Qwen分词器
> 千问分词器（官方标准名：Qwen Tokenizer）是阿里云通义千问团队，为 Qwen 系列大语言模型（含你文中的 Qwen2.5）配套研发的文本分词工具，是大语言模型文本处理的核心前置模块。
> 
> 它的核心职能是完成文本与模型可计算信号的双向转换：
> - 编码：将人类可读的自然语言文本，拆分为最小语义单元（token），并映射为模型可计算的整数 ID 序列；
> - 解码：将模型输出的 token ID 序列，反向还原为自然语言文本。
> 
> #### Qwen2.5 分词器的核心技术特性
> **1.基于优化的 BPE 算法，专业场景适配性强**  
> 它采用大模型行业主流的字节对编码（Byte Pair Encoding, BPE） 算法构建，原生词表规模为 151936，全> 面覆盖中文、英文、多语言、代码、特殊符号，尤其针对中文语义做了专项优化，分词准确率和语义完整性远优于通用分词器，完美适配机器人指令、推理文本等专业场景。
> 
> **2.与 Qwen 预训练权重深度绑定，保障训练稳定性**  
> 大语言模型的分词器与模型的词嵌入层（Embedding Layer）是一一对应的，词表必须完全匹配才能正常加载预训练权重。你文中用 Qwen2.5-1.5B 初始化三个专家模块，配套使用原生 Qwen2.5 分词器，能最大化保留模型的预训练语义理解能力，避免分词不匹配导致的权重失效、训练崩溃，正好对应文中开头 **“为提升训练稳定性”** 的核心设计目标。
> 
> **3.原生支持词表灵活拓展，适配自定义流程控制**  
> 该分词器支持在原生词表基础上，新增自定义的特殊控制 token，无需重构整个分词体系。这也是你文中核心的设计用法：通过新增⟨think start⟩、⟨vision start⟩、⟨action start⟩等特殊 token 拓展词表，让分词器将这些符号识别为独立的、不可拆分的语义单元，给模型传递明确的阶段切换信号，严格划分文本推理、视觉生成、动作执行三个流程，实现结构化、可解释的模型工作流。


## 3.3. EM-CoT Data Pipeline
To support the generation of intermediate textual reasoning trace r and visual subgoal ˆ ot+h, we construct an automatic
pipeline to synthesize EM-CoT data from raw robot trajectories at scale. As shown in Figure 2, the pipeline operates in
three phases. Firstly, we translate continuous low-level actions into high-level motion primitives via rule-based matching
following Belkhale et al. (2024). Secondly, we utilize a large-scale Vision-Language Model (Qwen3-VL (Bai et al., 2025))
to augment the trajectory with dense textual reasoning, including task narratives and subtask decomposition. Finally, the
terminal frame of each subtask is designated as its corresponding visual subgoal, providing a sparse supervision signal that
effectively lowers the learning difficulty. This process yields a high-quality dataset Dft where every trajectory is augmented
with aligned textual reasoning r and visual goals ˆ o, enabling the supervision of the intermediate steps defined in Eq. (1)-(2).
See Appendix B for more details on this pipeline, including the pseudo code and tailored prompt used in each phase.

为支撑模型生成中间文本推理链路 $r$ 与视觉子目标 $\hat{o}_{t+h}$ ，我们构建了一套自动化流水线，可基于原始机器人轨迹大规模合成具身多模态思维链（EM-CoT）数据。如图2所示，该流水线分为三个执行阶段。

第一阶段，我们参考Belkhale等人（2024）的方法，通过基于规则的匹配策略，将连续的底层动作转化为高层运动基元。第二阶段，我们采用大规模视觉语言模型（Qwen3-VL，Bai等人，2025），为轨迹补充稠密的文本推理内容，包括任务叙述与子任务拆解。最后，我们将每个子任务的末端帧设定为其对应的视觉子目标，以此提供稀疏监督信号，有效降低模型的学习难度。

通过该流程，我们构建得到高质量数据集 $\mathcal{D}_{ft}$ ，数据集中的每条轨迹都补充了对齐的文本推理  $r$ 与视觉目标 $\hat{o}$ ，能够为公式(1)-(2)中定义的中间步骤提供监督信号。关于该流水线的更多细节，包括各阶段所使用的伪代码与定制化提示词，详见附录B。

- **AP (Imitation Learning)**: We repurpose the OXE dataset for direct action prediction. The model learns to predict the next action chunk $a_t$ given the history, supervised by an $L_1$ flow-matching loss, $\mathcal{L}_\text{L1}$.

To address the varying optimization difficulties across these tasks, we balance the pre-training objective by assigning higher weights to manipulation intensive tasks. The total pre-training loss is defined as:

$$
\mathcal{L}_\text{pt} = 0.25\mathcal{L}_\text{CE} + 0.5\mathcal{L}_\text{MSE} + \mathcal{L}_\text{L1}. \tag{4}
$$

**Stage 2: EM-CoT-Augmented Fine-tuning.** Building upon the pre-trained foundation, we transition HALO from a reactive policy to a deliberative one by fine-tuning it on our EM-CoT dataset, $\mathcal{D}_\text{ft}$ (see Sec. 3.3). This stage augments standard robotic trajectories with explicit textual reasoning traces $\mathbf{r}$ and visual subgoals $\hat{\mathbf{o}}$.

Adhering to the sequential structure defined in Eq. (1)–(3), the model is trained to orchestrate a complete "thought-foresight-action" chain. The fine-tuning objective minimizes the joint loss:

$$
\mathcal{L}_\text{ft} = \mathcal{L}_\mathbf{r} + \mathcal{L}_\hat{\mathbf{o}} + \mathcal{L}_\mathbf{a}, \tag{5}
$$

where $\mathcal{L}_\mathbf{r}$, $\mathcal{L}_\hat{\mathbf{o}}$, and $\mathcal{L}_\mathbf{a}$ represent the losses for textual reasoning, visual subgoal generation, and action prediction, respectively. To mitigate catastrophic forgetting of general multimodal reasoning capabilities during this specialization, we incorporate general VQA data into the fine-tuning corpus (Cheang et al., 2025). This process results in a model that effectively bridges high-level semantic intent with precise, visually grounded motor control for long-horizon manipulation. ## 4.1. Experiment Settings
During pre-training, following (Deng et al., 2025), training samples are concatenated to a maximum sequence length of 27k
tokens, and Flex Attention (Dong et al., 2024) is adopted to accelerate training; HALO is trained for 90k steps at this stage.
For fine-tuning, we use raw trajectories collected from RoboTwin 2.0 (Chen et al., 2025c) and real-world. The simulation
dataset contains 2,500 expert demonstrations (50 per task) collected in clean environments, while the real-world dataset
consists of 320 demonstrations (80 per task). Using these datasets, HALO is fine-tuned for 110k steps in simulation and 80k
steps in real-world experiments. During inference, task instructions together with c = 3 observation frames are provided as
input. We set the action chunk length to K = 16 for simulation and K = 50 for real-world experiments.

## 4.1 实验设置
预训练阶段，参照文献 (Deng et al., 2025) 的设置，我们将训练样本拼接至最大序列长度 27k 词元，同时采用 Flex Attention 机制 (Dong et al., 2024) 加速训练；本阶段 HALO 模型共完成 9 万步训练。

微调阶段，我们采用从 RoboTwin 2.0 数据集 (Chen et al., 2025c) 与真实世界场景中采集的原始轨迹数据。其中仿真数据集包含在纯净无干扰环境中采集的 2500 条专家演示数据（单任务 50 条），真实世界数据集包含 320 条专家演示数据（单任务 80 条）。基于上述数据集，我们在仿真环境中对 HALO 完成 11 万步微调，在真实世界实验中完成 8 万步微调。

推理阶段，模型的输入为任务指令与 c=3 帧连续观测图像。我们将仿真实验的动作块长度设为 K=16，真实世界实验的动作块长度设为 K=50。


## 4.2. Simulation Results
We conduct simulation experiments on RoboTwin 2.0 (Chen et al., 2025c), a comprehensive benchmark comprising 50
challenging manipulation tasks. We compare HALO with several competitive baselines, including π0 (Black et al., 2024),
RDT (Liu et al., 2024b), and Diffusion Policy (Chi et al., 2025). Baseline results are taken directly from the official
RoboTwin 2.0 leaderboard.1 To isolate the contribution of EM-CoT, we further include a variant of our method without
EM-CoT, denoted as HALO-w/o EM-CoT.

## 4.2 仿真实验结果
我们在 RoboTwin 2.0 基准数据集 (Chen et al., 2025c) 上开展了仿真实验，该数据集是一套包含 50 项高难度操作任务的综合测试基准。我们将 HALO 与多个主流 SOTA 基线模型进行对比，包括 π0 (Black et al., 2024)、RDT (Liu et al., 2024b) 与扩散策略（Diffusion Policy, Chi et al., 2025），所有基线模型的结果均直接取自 RoboTwin 2.0 官方排行榜 ¹。为单独验证 EM-CoT 机制的性能贡献，我们额外设置了移除 EM-CoT 的模型变体，记为 HALO-w/o EM-CoT。

Table 1 shows the performance of HALO and all baselines on RoboTwin 2.0. It can be observed that HALO consistently
outperforms all competitive baselines across both Easy and Hard settings. The average success rate of HALO reaches 80.46%
on Easy tasks and 26.44% on Hard tasks, surpassing baseline policy π0 by 34.1% and 10.1%, respectively. Particularly, the
consistent huge relative performance gap (i.e., 73.5% and 62.0%) between HALO and π0 especially on Hard tasks indicates
that HALO can also handle out-of-distribution (OOD) challenges more robustly. This stems from the fact that HALO not only
possesses robust foundational capabilities but also features EM-CoT capabilities meticulously tailored for intricate, complex
scenarios. Besides, even without the explicit reasoning chain, HALO-w/o EM-CoT already surpasses the strongest baseline
(π0) by a substantial margin of +28.92 points on average for Easy tasks, demonstrating the powerful general foundation
established by our versatile pre-training.

表 1 展示了 HALO 与所有基线模型在 RoboTwin 2.0 基准上的性能表现。可以看到，在简易（纯净）与困难（域随机化）两种测试设置下，HALO 的性能均持续优于所有主流基线模型。HALO 在简易任务上的平均成功率达到 80.46%，在困难任务上达到 26.44%，分别较基线策略 π0 高出 34.1 个百分点与 10.1 个百分点。尤其值得注意的是，HALO 与 π0 之间存在持续的大幅相对性能差距（相对提升幅度分别为 73.5% 与 62.0%），在困难任务上的优势尤为突出，这表明 HALO 能够以更强的鲁棒性应对分布外（OOD）挑战。这一优势源于：HALO 不仅具备鲁棒的基础能力，还搭载了专为复杂精细场景深度定制的 EM-CoT 推理能力。此外，即使移除了显式推理链，HALO-w/o EM-CoT 在简易任务上的平均性能仍以 28.92 个百分点的大幅优势超越了最强基线 π0，充分验证了我们提出的多能预训练方案所构建的强大通用能力基底。

success rates dropping to near-zero (0.6%) in Hard settings. While π0 and RDT-1B exhibit some degree of robustness, they
plateau at significantly lower performance levels, whereas HALO continues to improve as it incorporates more structured
reasoning. On fine-grained tasks such as “Blocks Ranking Size” and “Stamp Seal,” HALO achieves a multi-fold increase
in success rates compared to baselines, demonstrating its ability to manage intricate object interactions. These results
underscore the effectiveness and robustness of HALO for complex robotic manipulation.

5. Conclusion
In this paper, we propose HALO, a unified VLA model that enables embodied multimodal chain-of-thought (EM-CoT)
reasoning through joint textual reasoning, visual foresight, and action prediction using a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT)
architecture. We also introduce an automated pipeline for synthesizing EM-CoT training data, along with a carefully
crafted training recipe. Extensive experiments and ablation studies validate the effectiveness of HALO in the challenging
out-of-distribution and real-world settings, highlighting HALO as a scalable and generalizable human-like VLA reasoning
paradigm.

A.2. Multimodal Encoders
To jointly train all three modalities, we implement different encoders to map multimodal inputs into tokens, stated as follows:
• Textual Input: We utilize Qwen2.5’s original tokenizer (Bai et al., 2023).
• Visual Input: We employ separate visual encoders to handle visual understanding and generation independently. For visual
understanding, we utilize a ViT (Dosovitskiy, 2020) encoder initialized with SigLIP2-so400m/14 (Tschannen et al., 2025)
(pre-trained at 384×384). To enhance high-resolution perception, we interpolate position embeddings to support inputs
up to 980×980 and integrate NaViT (Dehghani et al., 2023) to process images in their native aspect ratios. A two-layer
MLPprojector is adopted to align visual features with LLM’s embedding space. For visual generation, we leverage the
pre-trained VAE (Kingma & Welling, 2013) from FLUX (Labs et al., 2025), featuring a downsample ratio of 8 and a latent
channel of 16. The VAE latents are processed by flattening 2×2 spatial patches into vectors, which are then projected by a
linear layer to match the hidden dimension of the LLM. Conversely, a VAE decoder is employed similarly to reconstruct
images from latents. All VAE parameters remain frozen during training.
• Action Input: We employ a simple yet effective linear projection layer as the action encoder, mapping the raw continuous
actions directly into the LLM’s hidden dimension. Symmetrically, a linear decoder is utilized to project the LLM’s output
hidden states back to the original action dimensions for execution.

B.2. Prompts for EM-CoT
Our annotation pipeline employs a three-stage prompting strategy to transform observations into structured, high-level task
descriptions. First, a VLM generates a coherent, temporally-ordered narrative of the entire task. Second, this narrative is
decomposed into a sequence of goal-oriented subtasks. Finally, each video frame is aligned to its corresponding subtask,
with the model providing first-person reasoning that links the observed actions to the overall plan. This staged approach
ensures temporal coherence and semantic clarity in the final annotations.

E. Details on Real-World Experiment
Our real-world experiments are all conducted with COBOT Magic, a robot using the Mobile ALOHA system design (Fu
et al., 2024) and manufactured by AgileX Robotics. Notably, we exclusively utilize the robot’s head camera as the sole
source of visual input, requiring the model to reason and act based on an egocentric perspective. To evaluate policies, we
design a set of tasks that span various dimensions, including:
• Tool-mediated Manipulation: Gently pick up the broom and sweep the buttons on the board into the dustpan. (”Sweep
the buttons”)
• Semantic Visual Grounding: Place the grey cup into the pink cup with left gripper, and the green cup into the yellow cup
with right gripper. (”Bimanual cup nesting”)
• Inter-arm Collaboration: Handover the screwdriver from the left to the right. (”Handover the screwdriver”)
• Multi-step Manipulation: Open the top drawer, put the lemon on the plate into the top drawer, close the drawer. (”Put
lemon into drawer”)
Wecollect 80 trajectories for each task, and process them through our automated pipeline to yield the EM-CoT data. We
then aggregate the data across all four tasks into a multi-task corpus for finetuning.
In order to evaluate generalization, we design settings that span various axes of unseen scenarios, including visual distraction,
lightning variation, background variation, and novel object. The generalization settings are formulated as follows.
• Visual Distraction: Randomly placing irrelevant objects such as Coke bottle, cups onto the table.
• Lightning Variation: Varying the illumination levels of a desk lamp to introduce visual interference.
• Background Variation: Changing the color of the tablecloth.
• Novel Object: For the task of sweeping the buttons, we change the broom to a sponge during evaluation.
